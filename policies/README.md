# Policy content for the bdoor platform

See `BDoor_Legal_Policy_Content_Pack_EN_2026-08-29.md` for the owner-supplied
English working drafts (version 0.9, 29 August 2026).

Status rules:

- Every policy ships as **Draft** until counsel and compliance approve it.
- Draft pages show the substantive text with a clear draft banner and `noindex`.
- Bangla routes show the full English draft plus a translation-review notice
  until a professional Bangla translation is approved.
- `LEGAL_CONTENT_STATUS` / `LEGAL_LAUNCH_APPROVED` must stay `draft` / `false`
  until the launch checklist in the content pack is complete.

Do not invent addresses, registration numbers, liability caps, officer names,
providers, or government affiliations when wiring these documents into the app.
