# bdoor typography

## Primary Latin font

Use **Manrope** for website, app, presentation and marketing text.

- Display headings: Manrope 700
- Section headings: Manrope 650 or 700
- Buttons and navigation: Manrope 600
- Body copy: Manrope 400 or 500
- Captions and metadata: Manrope 500

Official source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope

## Bengali font

Use **Noto Sans Bengali** for Bangla interfaces and communications.

- Bangla headings: Noto Sans Bengali 700
- Bangla body: Noto Sans Bengali 400 or 500

Official source: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Bengali

## Logo wordmark

The supplied SVG wordmark has been converted to vector outlines. Do not recreate the logo by typing `bdoor` in Manrope or another font.

Font files are not redistributed in this package. Download them from the official sources and follow their licence terms.
