# bdoor brand package

**Release:** Version 1.0, August 2026

**Tagline:** Your door to business in Bangladesh.

This package contains the approved bdoor identity system: a four-colour geometric mark inspired by Bangladesh's rickshaw colour culture, paired with a precise lowercase wordmark.

## Quick selection

- Use `01_Logos/SVG/bdoor-primary-horizontal.svg` for websites and print.
- Use `01_Logos/PDF/bdoor-primary-horizontal.pdf` for print workflows that require PDF artwork.
- Use `01_Logos/PNG/bdoor-primary-horizontal-transparent.png` where SVG is unsupported.
- Use `01_Logos/SVG/bdoor-primary-reversed.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use `01_Logos/SVG/bdoor-monochrome-midnight.svg` for one-colour documents.
- Use `02_Icons/bdoor-app-icon-1024.png` for app-store and profile preparation.
- Use `02_Icons/favicon.ico` for the website favicon.
- Read `05_Brand_Guidelines/BDoor_Brand_Guidelines.pdf` before external production.
- See `00_README/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` for the complete deliverables list.

## Brand spelling

- Logo: `bdoor`
- Marketing copy: `bdoor`
- Domain: `bdoor.io`
- Do not write `BDOOR`, `B Door`, `B-Door` or `bDoor`.

## Logo rules

- Maintain clear space equal to the width of the red upper panel around the logo.
- Minimum horizontal logo width: 120 px digital or 32 mm print.
- Minimum symbol size: 24 px digital or 7 mm print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour individual pieces, add effects, or place the colour logo over a busy photograph.
- Prefer the colour logo on white or Cloud. Use the reversed logo on Midnight.

## Notes

- Legal, privacy and government-service claims are not part of the visual identity and require professional review.
- Confirm trademark clearance before final commercial registration.
- The four-colour symbol should remain fixed. Use the wider rickshaw palette only in illustrations and campaigns.
