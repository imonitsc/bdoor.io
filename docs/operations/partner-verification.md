# Partner verification

Partner organisations apply, upload credentials, enrol MFA, and await staff verification (`partner_verification_status`).

Until Gate C closes:

- Do not advertise named verified partners on marketing pages
- Partner enquiry flow remains available
- Assigned case work may proceed in staging with fictional seed partners only

Partners must not see unassigned cases, other firms’ quotations, platform-wide exports, or compliance decisions outside permission.

Mandatory MFA for `partner_owner` and `partner_staff`.
